A numerical phantom, transformed and then masked by what has been measured. Everything unmeasured is left at zero, so the image sharpens as the outer lines arrive and gaps in the coverage show up as the aliasing they really cause.

The measured k-space itself, on a log scale fixed by the fully sampled data so it does not rescale as samples arrive. Black is not measured. The bright centre carries the contrast; the edges, orders of magnitude weaker, carry the detail.
