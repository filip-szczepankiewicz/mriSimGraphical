Number of spin packets

Few packets: you can follow individual arrows, but the net vector is noisy. Many: a smooth signal that matches the analytic envelope.

Mechanisms

T1 · spin–lattice

Energy exchange with the lattice. Pushes Mz back to M0, and shortens the packets as they stand up.

T2 · spin–spin

Randomly fluctuating local fields from neighbouring spins. Each packet wanders in phase — irreversible. This is the spin–spin contribution alone: T1 always adds R1/2 on top, so the T2 in the readout can never exceed 2·T1 however far left you drag this.

  ↳ fluctuation memory τc

How long a local field keeps its value. At τc = 0 the kicks are white noise and the arrows shiver; longer τc gives the same long-time T2 with a much calmer picture. Keep it well below the echo time: a field that is still remembered at the 180° is partly refocused too, which is real (it is why CPMG works) but muddies the story.

T2* · ΔB0 spread

Static field inhomogeneity. Each packet keeps a fixed offset frequency and fans out predictably — a 180° pulse brings it back.
